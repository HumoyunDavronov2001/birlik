# Code of conduct

BIRLIK follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming to people of every background and level of experience; criticize ideas,
not people; no harassment, insults or personal attacks — in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else the
project is represented.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/HumoyunDavronov2001). Reports are handled confidentially, and
maintainers may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
