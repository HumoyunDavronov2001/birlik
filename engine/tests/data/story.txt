Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house near a big green forest. Every morning, Lily liked to look out of her window and watch the birds fly over the trees. One day, she saw a tiny blue bird sitting on the fence. The bird looked sad because it could not find its mother.

Lily ran outside and said, "Hello, little bird. Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I lost my mom. I do not know where she went." Lily wanted to help. She picked up the bird very gently and held it in her hands. "Do not worry," she said. "We will find your mom together."

They walked into the forest. The trees were tall and the grass was soft. They saw a rabbit eating a carrot and a squirrel climbing a tree. Lily asked the rabbit, "Have you seen a blue bird?" The rabbit thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I saw a blue bird by the river. She was calling for her baby."

Lily and the little bird went to the river. The water was cool and it made a happy sound. Near a big rock, they saw a blue bird looking all around. The little bird chirped loudly. The big bird turned and flew to them. It was the mother! She hugged her baby with her wings and they were very happy.

The mother bird said, "Thank you, Lily. You are very kind." Lily smiled and waved goodbye. She walked home and told her mom about the birds. Her mom gave her a big hug and said, "I am proud of you." That night, Lily went to sleep with a smile on her face, and she dreamed of flying with the birds over the green forest.

The next day, Tom came to play with Lily. Tom was her best friend. He had a red ball and a big box of toys. They played in the garden all morning. They threw the ball and ran after it. Then they built a tall tower with blocks. The tower fell down and they laughed and laughed. Tom said, "Let us build it again, but bigger!" They worked together and made the biggest tower ever. When it was time for lunch, Lily's mom made sandwiches and gave them cold milk. Tom said thank you and Lily said thank you too. After lunch, they sat under the tree and shared a book about a brave little dog who saved a cat from the rain.
