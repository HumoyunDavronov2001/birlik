01 · Home screen — the Ring

See the whole cluster at a glance

Every device is an arc on the ring. Its length is the share of model layers it holds, and its color is its current load. Tokens travel around the ring at their real, measured speed, so a slow device or a slow link stands out instantly. Several dots move at once — that is continuous speculative decoding.

07 · Design system

One source — three platforms

Tokens are built from a single JSON file (W3C DTCG) into CSS, Swift and Kotlin. The colors come from Samarkand's tilework: lapis, turquoise, gold and ganch plaster. There is one accent — turquoise; the rest of the interface stays neutral.
